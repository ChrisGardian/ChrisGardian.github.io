## The concept

The player has to reach the **black door** before the timer hits **2 minutes**, without touching the **lasers** or the **lava**. The best times are saved to a **high-score table** available from the main menu.

## What I built

- **Gameplay**: character movement and jumping, timer, damage from traps, end of game.
- **Level design**: the course, platform and trap placement.
- **All 3D assets**, modeled in Blender: platforms, street lamps, laser turrets, props.
- **UI**: main menu, high-score table, return to menu.
